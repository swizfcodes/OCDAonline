Oyin-Akoko, built by its own sons and daughters.

OCDA brings Oyin indigenes at home and abroad together with Kabiyesi, the Oloyin of Oyin-Akoko, to plan, fund and deliver projects for our town.

What the association does

One platform for every well-meaning Oyin indigene to work together on the growth of the town.

Read about OCDA

Mobilise

Bring community stakeholders together around shared priorities for the town.

Fund projects

Initiate development projects and pay for them through members' dues and levies.

Engage

Work with local authorities and the traditional council on behalf of the community.

Unite

Keep Oyin indigenes connected and working together, wherever they live.

Empower

Promote education, health and economic opportunity in Oyin-Akoko.
